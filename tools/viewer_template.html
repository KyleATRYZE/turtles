    The rooms

    Smelting hall 8 × 29

    Inside
    12 furnaces along the west wall, each on the wired network. The two chimneys rise from here.
    How it works
    crafter1 splits every smelting job across all the furnaces at once: cobblestone to stone, sand to glass, ores to ingots, logs to charcoal. Under the current single furnace, the building's smelting would take {{SMELT_HOURS_1}} hours; the bank does it in about {{SMELT_MIN_13}} minutes.
    Needs
    12 furnaces, modems and cable. The factory already splits its smelting over every furnace it can see.

    Storage room 8 × 29

    Inside
    60 double chests three high down both walls (3,240 slots to start), each pair wired in behind; a front desk at the south end with an In chest, an Out chest, the sorting computer and a 5 × 3 touch screen.
    How it works
    Anything dropped in the In chest is sorted into colour-coded sections; ask on the screen or in chat and it lands in the Out chest. The chests can be upgraded in place to Sophisticated Storage tiers later for far more room. Full proposal: the storage plan page.
    Needs
    Being redrawn: Sophisticated Storage chests and barrels joined to one controller in place of the 120 chests, and the storage program.

    Launch bay 8 × 29

    Inside
    At the back, on the drone lane: the pit stop (in-shaft, drop-off chest, crafter1, launch pad, out-shaft) and the drone rack, 16 homes in two rows with copper pads and a glowing name board over each, seen through bars from the bay. The rest of the bay is clear floor behind the big arched door, with a 2 × 2 touch panel by the door.
    How it works
    Miners come up the in-shaft, drop their load, get serviced over crafter1 and drop down the out-shaft, all without leaving the lane. A drone with nothing to do slides into its slot in the rack. Turn on Drone routes above to see the lot.
    Needs
    Moves over last, once the rest works, so mining never stops. The miners switch to the new base on their next trip.

    Drone lane 45 × 2, along the back wall

    Inside
    A two-track lane the full length of the building behind a screen of iron bars, on a yellow and black hazard-striped floor. Gates in both end walls, each with an IN and an OUT light over its tracks; portholes through the partitions.
    How it works
    The back track runs west and the front track runs east, so drones never meet head-on and never cross a room. The gate lights flash while a drone goes through. Everything the drones visit is on the lane: the pit stop, the rack, the farm hatch.
    Needs
    88 striped yellow concrete (in stock, from Kyle), 102 iron bars, 4 small monitors for the lights.

    Mekanism foundry 16 × 29, double height

    Inside
    The bay that was going to be a tree farm. A brick plinth along the east wall carries the machine line, a second plinth in the corner carries the power, and a tiled lane runs in front of the machines, all under the glazed sawtooth roof.
    How it works
    The swarm builds the machines itself, starting from a Metallurgic Infuser and a Heat Generator, and a turtle of its own (foundry1) feeds and empties them. The machine layout is drawn in once the first powered run has shown how they behave.
    Needs
    Osmium, iron, copper, redstone and coal, all in stock. Wind generators on the roof later, so it runs on no fuel.

    Builder workshop 26 × 29, upper floor

    Inside
    mapper1's home: where materials for the next job are laid out, and where it parks. A glass wall looks down over the foundry.
    How it works
    Spare floor space for storage to grow into, and for a second builder if one helps.
    Needs
    Nothing extra: it comes with the shell.

    Control tower 11 × 11, 24 tall

    Inside
    Top floor: a 9 × 4 wall of advanced monitors, CommandBlock with the Chat Box and Player Detector, a 3 × 2 touch panel beside it, and a big window looking south over the site. Lower floors: the other computers, reached by ladder.
    How it works
    The monitor wall shows the live fleet map, storage levels and the factory's queue. The touch panel has the buttons: recall everyone, resume, pause the build, and a button per drone (recall, send to work, find on the map).
    Needs
    42 advanced monitors (about 75 gold), a dashboard program, and CommandBlock moved in.

    Materials

    Counted block by block from the model, then traced back through the crafting to what the miners dig. Deepslate needs only crafting; stone bricks, glass and copper need smelting: {{SMELTS}} furnace jobs in all.

    Sand is the one thing the swarm cannot find: about 475 for the glass. A desert or beach run, or a crusher turning cobblestone into sand, covers it.

    Build order

    Each phase leaves everything working, so the swarm keeps mining throughout. The tools come first, because a builder that can't fetch its own materials would need you at every step.

    1. 0

      Gather and map

      Done
      • Miners haul two stacks each of cobblestone and cobbled deepslate home every trip.
      • mapper1 maps the meadow, so the building can be placed on flat ground.
      • Coalition keeps the coal coming.

      You: if you can spare them, put a few extra furnaces on the network. Smelting is the slow part.

    2. 1

      Teach the turtles to build

      Done
      • crafter1: smelt on every furnace at once; craft in bulk (deepslate to polished, bricks and tiles; stairs; panes; cut copper); take material orders.
      • mapper1: gets a pickaxe (3 diamonds, in stock); learns build mode: clear and level the site, then place the plan layer by layer, collecting each load of materials from the factory and facing stairs the right way.
      • Each tested in simulation first, then on a small trial build next to the hut.
    3. 2

      The shell

      Running now
      • Level the site and lay the floor. A dirt bank sloping down round the south and east sides hides the foundation.
      • Windows go in with the walls: glass panes set into the brickwork for depth.
      • Walls and pillars to the first floor, the upper floor slab, walls to the eaves.
      • Sawtooth roof, chimneys and the control tower.

      About 9,300 placements. mapper1 works through it in loads of about 900 blocks; a second builder would halve the time.

    4. 3

      Smelting hall

      Planned
      • Furnace bank and network, wired back to the factory's chests. Everything after this goes faster.
    5. 4

      Storage room

      Planned
      • The drone lane: hazard floor, screen, gate lights and portholes, so the rooms stay clear from here on.
      • Chest walls and network (mapper1 right-clicks each modem on with the automata core).
      • Storage program: sorting, search screen, the door chest.
      • Today's chests and their contents move in.
    6. 5

      Control tower

      Planned
      • Monitor wall and dashboard program; CommandBlock, the Chat Box and the Player Detector move in.

      You: carry CommandBlock across (computers keep their programs when broken and placed).

    7. 6

      Mekanism foundry

      Started
      • Floor and plinths; then the machine line, built and run by the swarm: infuser, steel and circuits, enrichment chamber, smelter, cable, wind power.

      You: nothing, unless the tests say otherwise.

    8. 7

      Move the launch bay

      Last
      • New shafts down to fresh roads at the back of the bay, then crafter1, the pad and the drop-off move into the pit stop; the drones move into the rack.
      • Miners switch base on their next trip; their plots move to around the new building.

      You: carry crafter1 across.

    9. 8

      Finishing touches

      Last
      • 24 lanterns, hung where a lighting check says they're needed so nothing can spawn inside: on chains under the tall roofs, about five blocks off the floor. Copper pipe runs, a path back to the hut.
      • An inspection pass: mapper1 scans the finished building, compares it with this plan and fixes anything missing or out of place.
