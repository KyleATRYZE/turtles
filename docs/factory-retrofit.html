Factory retrofit

Where everything ends up once the base moves into the factory. The drones come and go by the drone lane along the back wall and dock in racks facing the lane, where their loads come in over the cable (loading bays): no drone carries anything inside the base. The foundry's machines are worked over the cable too. Hover over any block for what is there; switch the drone routes on and off.
