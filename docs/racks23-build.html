Racks 2 and 3

Homes for every other drone, built the same way as rack 1: rack 2 (x 35-42, 16 homes) for the 11 chunk loaders, with 5 spare for more helpers; rack 3 (x 28-31, 8 homes) for the miners and BlockParty. Each middle shelf is a row of full-block wired modems; a monitor strip behind it keeps them switched on and shows who is home. Cable only under the lane floor and inside the posts.
