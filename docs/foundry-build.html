The foundry on the cable

Ore, alloys, circuits and steel without a drone carrying anything: every machine on the plinth sits on a wired modem in the floor, and the works computer moves items from the store straight into it and the results straight back. The modems are flush in the plinth (the machine covers each one); the cable runs a block under the floor.

      The right-clicks

      Each machine modem is clicked once; the machine on it keeps it switched on. The modem under the works computer is never clicked. Do the chamber's first and send probe Crafty: it tells whether the store can push into a Mekanism machine by name (docs/cc-lessons.md: it may not).

      #World x y zTouches

      If the probe says no, the machines are fed the way the piped line is: the inbox barrel (on the cable) is emptied by a logistical sorter into the first machine, the machines push their results on (side config, ejecting) and the last one into the outbox barrel (on the cable). Crafty fills the inbox and empties the outbox. Only steps 1 and 5 are needed then.
