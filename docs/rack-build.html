Rack 1: loading bays

What you build by hand so loads reach a docked drone over the cable instead of on a launch pad. Crafty pushes each load and its fuel straight into the drone in its home, and takes hand-ins back the same way. Nothing sticks out: every modem is a full block that is part of the shelf or the wall, and every cable is under the floor or inside the wall.

The rack wall in 3D

Drag sideways (or use the buttons) to turn it. Seen from the launch bay by default: the lane is behind the rack, the storage room to the left. New blocks are bright; existing ones are pale. Hover a block for its world coordinates.

    Each slice from above, north up (the lane at the top), x running east. One square is one block. y -1 is under the floor; y 0 is the floor you walk on. Only x 0-28, z 1-5 is shown: the hall's corner on the left, the launch bay on the right.

    Steps

    Click a step to light up its blocks above.

      The right-clicks

      Only the eight shelf modems are clicked, once each, after the strip is up (the strip is what keeps each of them switched on when no drone is docked). One modem serves two homes, the one below it and the one on it, so eight is all there is: it can't be fewer, as each home touches only its own shelf block. The connector modems and the floor plug are never clicked.
