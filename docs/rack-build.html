Rack 1: loading bays

What you build by hand so loads reach a docked drone over the cable instead of on a launch pad. The store (and Crafty, once it is on the same cable) pushes each load and its fuel straight into the drone in its home, and takes hand-ins back the same way. Nothing sticks out: every modem is a full block that is part of the shelf or the wall, and every cable is under the floor or inside the wall.

      The right-clicks

      Only the eight shelf modems are clicked, once each, after the strip is up (the strip is what keeps each of them switched on when no drone is docked). One modem serves two homes, the one below it and the one on it, so eight is all there is: it can't be fewer, as each home touches only its own shelf block. The connector modems are never clicked.
